# Eczema Care App · Interactive Prototype

A clickable prototype of a mobile app that helps people with eczema track flare-ups, spot their triggers and calm the urge to scratch.

**[Try the live prototype →](https://monigonzalezux.github.io/eczema-app-prototype/)**

## What you can do in the prototype

- **Daily Log**: log your mood, active flare-ups on a body map, sleep and treatments
- **Track a flare-up**: mark where it is, how your skin feels and what you used on it
- **Itch Attack Panic**: a one-minute thumb game that keeps your hands busy until the urge passes
- **Tips and forecasts**: daily tips, trigger warnings and a symptom forecast with reminders
- **Skin Insights**: weekly flare-up chart and a body heat map of where it flares most
- **Calendar**: flare days at a glance, plus each day's log
- **Rewards**: points, streaks and trophies that reward consistent tracking

## About

Designed by Monica Gonzalez, UX/UI Designer.
